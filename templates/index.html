How the twin works

  1. It learns the rhythm of campus

    A year of hourly headcounts for every facility, joined with the academic calendar, weather and campus events. Exam weeks, rainy evenings and holidays all leave a pattern.

  2. Three models compete

    Ridge regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting are trained for each facility. The one with the lowest error on the most recent four weeks is kept.

  3. It gives advice, not just numbers

    The best time to go, what-if scenarios, a planner that orders your day around the crowds, and alerts for when your favourite place quietens down.

What you can do with it

Scrub through the day
Drag a slider and watch every building change colour hour by hour, today or any day this week.
Ask "what if?"
Switch on exam week, rain, a heatwave or a fest and see which places overflow.
Plan your day
Tell it what you need to do and how long each takes. It finds the least-crowded order and timing.
Report how it feels
One tap tells everyone the mess is packed. Fresh reports blend into the live estimate.
Get smart alerts
"Tell me when the gym drops below 30% in the next three hours." Predictive, not just live.
Inspect the model
See error rates, feature importance and predicted-versus-actual charts for every facility.
